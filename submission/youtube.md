Change one requirement, and Jira shows you which tests no longer prove it.

Tracebound is a Forge app for Jira for teams that keep requirements, risks and verification tests in Jira (medical-device, automotive and other regulated software). When a requirement or risk control is edited after its tests passed, Tracebound marks those tests SUSPECT, shows the word-by-word change, and lets the team create a re-verification task or clear it with a recorded reason. Clearances, tasks and settings go into a hash-chained audit log, and release baselines can be frozen and verified. The same checks are available as Rovo actions and MCP tools.
